## Outcome and scope

Of the original **29 target pages, 27 are published and 2 remain draft**.
The additional APS and DSU articles are also withheld. These are repository
publication decisions, not confirmation of a deployment or merge.

The site has **37 indexable editorial routes**: eight main/legal/directory
pages, seven destination guides, seventeen location guides and five articles.
The four drafts and the noindex utility 404 are excluded from discovery.
Every indexable route has a sitemap entry and inbound links. The utility 404
is not one of the 29 targets and deliberately has no canonical; all 41
editorial routes, including drafts, have self-canonicals.

Only opened official text on approved hosts counts as destination/article
verification. Search excerpts, missing pages, archived rules and an owner's
commercial statement do not establish a current official fact. Each retained
fact/FAQ is recorded with its URL, date and confirmed text in the source and
article journals. Historical blockers remain in flagged-items-for-verification.md;
the appended Phase 14 decisions supersede them only where expressly stated.

### Apache staging rewrite changes

**No .htaccess change in Phase 14.** Its CRLF bytes match the phase baseline.
The inherited Phase 13 canonical-sitemap rules remain:

```apache
RewriteCond %{THE_REQUEST} \s/+sitemap(?:\.php)?[\s?] [NC]
RewriteRule ^sitemap(?:\.php)?$ /sitemap.xml [R=301,L]
RewriteRule ^sitemap\.xml$ sitemap.php [END]
```

Before release, native Apache 2.4 staging must confirm the two redirects,
XML content type, no rewrite loop, clean PHP routes, query-string retention,
robots/canonical behavior and appropriate include/data/tool access controls.
The adapter's success does not prove these Apache behaviors. No main-branch
change, merge or deployment was performed.
