# Questions for ILMERA's owner

## How to reply

Please reply by question number. A sentence or short list is enough. You can say **"We do not offer this"** or **"Not sure; please do not publish a claim yet."** Both are useful answers. Do not guess a rule, price, result or qualification.

Questions 1-12 group all **29 unanswered prompts and 15 partly answered prompts** from Phase 20. Each of those 44 prompts appears in one main group. The six already answered prompts are not included. The groups are ordered by the number of related prompts, largest first.

The counts show questions and distinct pages already listed in the prompt map. They are not a promise that an owner reply will answer every prompt. Your reply can explain **what ILMERA does or does not do**. Admission, finance, visa and scholarship rules still need the sources listed at the end. Page counts overlap between groups and should not be added together.

**All example replies below are examples of wording, not statements about ILMERA. Use them only if accurate.** Fill in bracketed details yourself. No website content or proof will be added through this report.

## Business questions, in impact order

### 1. Admission and language checks

**Question:** What admission and language-evidence checks does ILMERA actually do for a student, and where does its help stop?

- **Why it matters:** Helps clarify 11 prompts: 1, 3, 4, 6, 27, 29, 30, 32, 33, 34 and 43. These touch 6 pages: Germany, UK, Services, the backlog guide, the documents guide and the choosing-a-consultant guide. We need your service boundaries, not country-by-country acceptance thresholds.
- **Example reply:** "We review the academic record and language evidence, discuss possible courses and check published requirements. The admissions office decides eligibility; we do not promise acceptance."
- **If you do not offer this:** "We do not assess admission or language eligibility; students must check with the admissions office."
- **If unsure:** "Please do not describe eligibility checking as our service until I confirm the exact tasks."

### 2. Loan, bank-account and finance help

**Question:** Which finance tasks, if any, does ILMERA handle for students?

- **Why it matters:** Helps clarify 9 prompts: 15, 35, 36, 37, 38, 39, 40, 41 and 42. These touch 4 pages: Lithuania, Germany, the cost guide and the documents guide. Please distinguish organising papers, introducing a lender, helping with an account and giving financial or tax advice. Your answer cannot establish loan approval or immigration funding rules.
- **Example reply:** "We help organise financial papers but do not arrange loans, recommend lenders, open accounts or give tax advice."
- **If you do not offer this:** "We do not offer loan, bank-account or tax assistance; students deal directly with the relevant provider."
- **If unsure:** "Please leave finance assistance unclaimed until I confirm our role and any referral arrangements."

### 3. Scholarship and Italy income-document help

**Question:** What scholarship tasks does ILMERA handle, including any help with Italy's DSU scholarship and ISEE family-income assessment papers?

- **Why it matters:** Helps clarify 6 prompts: 11, 12, 16, 21, 28 and 48, across 2 pages: the scholarships guide and the documents guide. We need to distinguish explaining an opportunity, checking papers, helping submit an application and obtaining official documents. Eligibility and award decisions are not ILMERA's to make.
- **Example reply:** "We identify published opportunities and help organise application papers. We do not issue income certificates, calculate an official family-income assessment or decide awards."
- **If you do not offer this:** "We do not handle DSU or ISEE paperwork; students must use the responsible body's instructions."
- **If unsure:** "Please do not describe a complete scholarship-document service until I confirm the tasks we actually handle."

### 4. Study budgets and country comparisons

**Question:** What does an ILMERA budget comparison include, and what must the family check or pay for separately?

- **Why it matters:** Helps clarify 5 prompts: 8, 9, 10, 13 and 17. These touch 4 pages: Lithuania, UK, Germany and the cost guide. This would explain the limits of our planning help, not establish a lowest-cost country, a complete budget or a course's financial return.
- **Example reply:** "We separate tuition, living costs, application expenses and our own service charges. Each estimate needs a date and source; families obtain current travel prices, and unconfirmed scholarships are not deducted."
- **If you do not offer this:** "We do not prepare complete budgets or rank destinations by cost."
- **If unsure:** "Please describe any figures as limited examples, not a complete quotation, until I confirm our budgeting service."

### 5. Visa support and its limits

**Question:** Which visa tasks does ILMERA handle, from checking papers to appointment guidance or help after a refusal?

- **Why it matters:** Helps clarify 4 prompts: 19, 22, 24 and 26, across 2 pages: Italy and the documents guide. We need to say who prepares, submits, books and follows up. An owner reply cannot establish visa jurisdiction, available appointments, approval or permission to reapply.
- **Example reply:** "We help check application papers and explain the published appointment process. Students use the official booking channel; we do not promise appointments or visas, and we do not handle refusal appeals."
- **If you do not offer this:** "We do not book appointments or handle refusal cases; students must use the official instructions."
- **If unsure:** "Please publish no claim about appointment booking, appeals or reapplication support until I confirm our scope."

### 6. Advice about work and staying after study

### 8. Written charges, payment and refunds
