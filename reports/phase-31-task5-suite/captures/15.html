Our Core Services

What ILMERA helps with

  • ILMERA reviews a student's eligibility, academic qualifications, English-language evidence and admission criteria.
  • ILMERA guides students on financial requirements, bank accounts and education loan information.
  • ILMERA helps students identify scholarships and guides them through applications and required documents, including Italy DSU scholarships.
  • ILMERA compares tuition fees and living expenses to help students choose study destinations.
  • ILMERA guides students with visa documentation, appointment procedures and refusal-related guidance.
  • ILMERA guides students on obtaining income certificates, MOI letters and other supporting documents.
  • ILMERA guides students through APS requirements and document preparation for Germany.
  • ILMERA offers counselling for students interested in studying medicine in Russia. Recognition and permission to practise are decided by the responsible authorities.

What ILMERA does not do

ILMERA does not provide advice on working during study or staying after study.

Universities, immigration authorities, scholarship bodies, lenders and banks make their own decisions. ILMERA does not decide admissions, visas, scholarships or loans and does not promise outcomes.

Lender selection, loan decisions and account opening remain with the student and the relevant provider. Tax and investment advice are outside ILMERA's scope.

Owner-confirmed service scope: 22 October 2026. This is not an update to official country rules.

Service charges and how ILMERA is paid

ILMERA charges the student a service charge between ₹3 lakh and ₹15 lakh, depending on destination and university. This is on top of the university's own fee.

ILMERA also receives payments from some partner universities.

ILMERA has a written service-fee and payment policy covering service inclusions and refund terms. The policy terms are not yet published on this site.

Before paying, families should ask for the policy terms in writing and confirm in writing which amounts are university fees and which are ILMERA's service charge.
