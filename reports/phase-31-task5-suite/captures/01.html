Our Story

Why ILMERA Study Abroad
Exists

ILMERA Study Abroad was founded on a simple premise: every deserving student should have a clear, honest, supported pathway to a world-class education, regardless of where they come from. As part of ILMERA Group, the consultancy is run by three named, contactable people rather than a call centre.

Explore our destinations and services, read the published guides, or meet the ILMERA team.

ILMERA guides academic review, applications, scholarship enquiries and visa-document preparation. Universities, immigration authorities, scholarship bodies, lenders and banks make their own decisions.

ILMERA Study Abroad guides students to universities across nine destinations: Germany, Italy, the United Kingdom, the Czech Republic, Lithuania, Latvia, Slovakia, Russia and the GCC. The consultancy specialises in students other consultancies turn away, including those with academic backlogs, a low percentage, or no IELTS, because those are exactly the profiles that Russia, Slovakia, Lithuania and Latvia routinely accept.

ILMERA is based in Coorg and works with students by phone, WhatsApp and video call, so a student in Madikeri, Virajpet, Kushalnagar or Somwarpet gets the same counsellor, the same country-by-country cost breakdown and the same honest eligibility check as a student anywhere else in India.

Serving Kodagu & Coorg

Study Abroad Guidance for Students in Coorg & Kodagu

ILMERA Study Abroad is based in Coorg and works with students across Kodagu district (Madikeri, Virajpet, Kushalnagar, Somwarpet and Ponnampet) by phone, WhatsApp and video call. The process is the same for every student regardless of location: academic review, admissions/application guidance, cost comparison, scholarship guidance and visa-document guidance through the same online channels.

Why students from Kodagu are underserved

Almost every overseas education consultancy that serves Kodagu is headquartered in Bangalore, Mysuru or Mangalore, with Coorg treated as a catchment area rather than a market. In practice that means a student in Virajpet is expected to take a three-to-five hour round trip for a first counselling session, and again for document collection. Specialised advice, such as which German public universities waive IELTS, whether a 58% aggregate qualifies for Italy's DSU, or what a PUC backlog actually rules out, is rarely available locally at all.

Our genuine connection to Kodagu

ILMERA is based in Coorg. CMO Fawaz BH lives and works in Kushal Nagar, Kodagu, where he also runs an independent SEO consultancy. In practice that means counselling in Kannada, Malayalam, Hindi or English, an understanding of PUC and Karnataka state board marksheets and how foreign admissions offices read them, and someone who knows the district rather than a call centre reading a script.

What Kodagu students most often ask about

ILMERA at a glance

ILMERA Study Abroad provides online-only counselling for Indian students, with no counselling premises. Services include academic and English-evidence review, admissions/application guidance, financial information, study-cost comparison, scholarship and supporting-document guidance, visa-document, appointment-procedure and refusal-related guidance, Germany APS guidance and Russia medicine counselling. ILMERA does not provide advice on working during study or staying after study. Responsible bodies make their own decisions; ILMERA does not promise outcomes. ILMERA charges the student a service charge on top of the university's own fee. ILMERA also receives payments from some partner universities.

  • ILMERA reviews a student's eligibility, academic qualifications, English-language evidence and admission criteria.
  • ILMERA guides students on financial requirements, bank accounts and education loan information.

  • ILMERA compares tuition fees and living expenses to help students choose study destinations.
