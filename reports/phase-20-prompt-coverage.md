## Added FAQs and existing-source provenance

### Prompt 18: `/blog/how-to-choose-study-abroad-consultant-karnataka`

**Which costs should parents separate before agreeing to counselling?**

Keep study costs and counselling charges separate. Check tuition, any semester contributions, living costs and the written service scope, charges and refund terms. Published country examples are not an individual quotation, and this site does not publish a complete service-specific fee schedule.

Existing-source basis: Cost article; selection guide sections 1 and 5 and existing fee FAQ.

### Prompt 43: `/blog/how-to-choose-study-abroad-consultant-karnataka`

**Can a comparison guarantee admission with low marks or backlogs?**

No. A comparison cannot establish that a particular academic record will be accepted. Present the complete record, check the actual programme requirements and compare the written counselling scope before choosing support. Counselling does not guarantee admission or a visa.

Existing-source basis: Backlog article existing eligibility FAQ; selection guide sections 1 and 2.

### Prompt 44: `/blog/how-to-choose-study-abroad-consultant-karnataka`

**Does online-only delivery itself establish that a consultant is trustworthy?**

No. An online model does not by itself verify the counsellor or promised service. Check the named contact, written scope, charges, privacy and refund terms, and look for evidence that can be checked independently. ILMERA counsels by phone, WhatsApp and video call, with no counselling premises. Counselling does not guarantee admission or a visa.

Existing-source basis: Selection guide ten checks, online-counselling FAQ, no-guarantee FAQ; Coorg process article.

### Prompt 9: `/study-in-uk`

**How should I assess a UK Master's against the Graduate visa dates?**

For the Master's context, GOV.UK lists a 2-year Graduate visa for applications on or before 31 December 2026 and 18 months from 1 January 2027. These dates alone do not establish the value of a particular course. Compare the actual costs, course objectives and applicable conditions before deciding. Source: https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa

Existing-source basis: Existing homepage UK language FAQ and its GOV.UK Graduate visa citation, reopened on 2026-10-16; cost article individual-budget guidance.
