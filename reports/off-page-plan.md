# Off-page plan for an online-only consultancy

Reviewed: 2026-10-16. This is a plan, not a claim that profiles, student
reviews or directory approvals already exist. No accounts were created,
submissions made or proof slots populated.

## Collect genuine feedback without manufacturing proof

1. Ask actual students who used the service whether they want to give honest
   feedback, including criticism. Do not imply there are past results when
   none are evidenced. No targets, invented counts or prepared praise.
2. Ask the same neutral question regardless of the expected sentiment. Do
   not offer rewards, select only satisfied people or pressure anyone.
3. Explain where feedback might appear. Obtain explicit permission for any
   on-site quotation and its display name. Keep identity/evidence private.
4. Record the original source, permission, wording and date. Do not convert
   an enquiry or offer letter into a placement, visa or award claim.
5. If the owner later approves an authentic quotation, use the empty proof
   slot process in `proof-slots.md`. Do not publish rating/review schema.
6. A complaint is a service issue to resolve, not something to hide or answer
   with invented praise. Do not promise a ranking benefit from a review.

## Google Business Profile: current online-only model is ineligible

Opened Google's official **Business eligibility and ownership guidelines**,
sections **Eligible businesses** and **Ineligible businesses**, on 2026-10-16:
https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en

The eligibility section requires physical customer contact during stated
hours. The ineligible examples explicitly include online-only businesses.
ILMERA's confirmed online-only counselling model therefore does not qualify.
Do not create a profile or invent a customer-facing location or visits.

Also opened **Guidelines for representing your business on Google**, opening
eligibility paragraph and **Address / Service-area businesses** sections:
https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en

A service-area profile is for a business that travels to customers, not a
workaround for online-only delivery. A mailing location without actual
operations does not supply eligibility. A co-working location has specific
staffing, signage and customer-reception conditions; none is asserted here.
No change to ILMERA's business model or address is proposed.

These are substantive opened-policy findings, not certification of raw HTTP
